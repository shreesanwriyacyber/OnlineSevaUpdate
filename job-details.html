Airport Authority of India AAI Manager & Junior Executive Recruitment 2026

Airports Authority of India (AAI) has released the notification for Manager and Junior Executive Recruitment 2026. Interested candidates can apply online from 08/08/2026 to 07/09/2026.

Important Dates

  • Application Begin : 08/08/2026
  • Last Date for Apply Online : 07/09/2026
  • Pay Exam Fee Last Date : 07/09/2026
  • Exam Date : As Per Schedule
  • Admit Card Available : Before Exam

Application Fee

  • General / OBC / EWS : ₹1000/-
  • SC / ST / PH : ₹0/-
  • Female Candidate : ₹0/-
  • Pay the Examination Fee Through Online / Offline Fee Mode Only.

Age Limit

  • Minimum Age : NA
  • Maximum Age : 27 Years for Junior Executive
  • Maximum Age : 32 Years for Manager Post
  • Age Relaxation Extra as per AAI Manager, Junior Executive Examination 2026 Rules.

Eligibility / Qualification

Junior Executive (Finance)

MBA with Finance / PG Diploma in Management with Specialization in Finance OR CA / CFA.

Junior Executive (Law)

Bachelor Degree in Law (3 Year / 5 Year) from any recognized University in India. Enrolled with Bar Council in India.

Junior Executive (Operations)

Bachelor Degree in Science with MBA OR Bachelor Degree in Engineering.

Junior Executive (Survey & Cartography)

BE / B.Tech Degree in Civil OR M.Sc Degree in Geography OR Master in Remote Sensing and Geographic Information System OR Master in Geo Informatics OR Master in Geomatics.

Manager (Engg-Civil)

BE / B.Tech Degree in Related Trade with 3 Year Experience.

Manager (Finance)

MBA with Finance Specialization OR PG Diploma in Management with Finance Specialization with 3 Year Experience.

Manager (Information Technology)

BE / B.Tech Degree in Computer Science / Engineering / IT OR MCA with 3 Year Experience.

Manager (Architecture)

Bachelor Degree in Architecture and registered with Council of Architecture with 3 Year Experience.

Manager (Law)

Bachelor Degree (3 Year / 5 Year) Law from any recognized University in India. Enrolled with Bar Council with 3 Year Experience.

Manager (Fire Services)

BE / B.Tech Degree in Fire Engineering / Mechanical Engineering / Automobile Engineering with 3 Year Experience.

Manager (Operations)

Bachelor Degree in Science B.Sc with MBA OR Bachelor Degree in Engineering with 3 Year Experience.

Manager (Commercial)

Bachelor Degree in Any Stream with MBA in Marketing Specialization OR Bachelor Degree in Engineering with 3 Year Experience.

Manager (Corporate Planning & Management Services)

Master Degree in Mathematical Statistics / Operation Research / Statistics / Applied Statistics / Economics with Statistics / Mathematics with Statistics with 3 Year Experience.

Manager (Corporate Communications)

Bachelor Degree in Mass Communication / Journalism / Literature (Hindi/English) and PG Degree / Diploma in Journalism / Mass Media / Mass Communication with 3 Year Experience.
